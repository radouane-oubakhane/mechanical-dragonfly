# Step 28 — Cycle-averaged flight dynamics

Run `python simulation/step_28_flight.py` to generate hover and forward flight GLB clips, CSV telemetry and a JSON report. Use `--mode hover` or `--mode forward`, `--duration 4`, `--mass 0.035` (kg), `--output` and `--telemetry-dir` to configure. Existing three clips remain unchanged; the two new flight clips are non-looping. Open the GLB in an animation-compatible viewer; select `flight_hover` or `flight_forward`. The model translates in metres, so the viewer may need zooming out. The prior three clips are retained at the original rest position.

The six-DOF rigid-body integrator assumes 35 g, diagonal inertia, center of mass at (-12,0,0) mm relative to chassis, air density 1.225 kg/m³ and effective lift coefficient 1.2. Wing projected surface area and span-squared moment are integrated from Step 13 cell geometry. Each wing produces cycle-averaged lift in body +Z. Quadratic drag opposes world velocity. An attitude controller and clipped minimum-norm allocation produce individual frequencies, with a 40 Hz bound. A separate idealized yaw torque is applied, not derived from wings. Wing and drive animation use the integrated phase of each calculated frequency. Other articulation tracks repeat prior authored motions. Body motion rotates about the assumed COM. No aerodynamic wake, unsteady lift, collision, motor power/torque validation, actual yaw actuator, sensor feedback, ground contact, or physical prototype validation. Phase-dependent lift and aerodynamic coupling are not modeled. The nominal equal-frequency lift estimate is not the torque-trimmed hover frequency. No claim of real-world flight capability.

`python tests/test_step_28.py` runs six tests using the prebuilt flight GLB. Optional plots: `python -m pip install matplotlib` then `python simulation/plot_step_28.py`. The PNG charts are included and matplotlib is not required for the physics or export. Full prior regression suite not rerun.
